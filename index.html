Reading the result

Spread is the standard deviation of what you have left. Lower is a more even burn. Range is the gap between your largest and smallest remaining pile. Both are the numbers the solver is actually trying to shrink.

Higher Levels [15 - 50]

For running a single level against whichever sets you like. Pick the level, the rarity, and tick the sets to craft from. Search by set name, season, or advanced material.

Sets that need an advanced material work fine here. That material is left out of the optimisation on purpose (there's nothing to trade it off against), but its total consumption is reported with your other materials afterwards.

There is no single best set of gear. Flat bonuses win when your march is small and percentage bonuses win once it is large, so the answer depends on the march you already have.

Getting started

  1. Hit + Add on a slot and pick every piece you are considering for it. You can select several at once.
  2. Set each piece's level and rarity. You can set a defaults under Options.
  3. Choose your situation, and tick the bonuses you already have.
  4. Hit Find best gear.

Finding your base march size

  1. Choose the march preset you use for this situation.
  2. Unequip all six pieces: helmet, chest, pants, boots, ring and weapon.
  3. Go to the march screen and read the march size it shows.
  4. That is your base march size.

Reading the result

You get a list of march size ranges, each with the gear that is best across that whole range. 0 to 68,215 means exactly that: anywhere in there, that combination gives you the biggest march.

The line under each range is what that gear adds up to, as +12.87% · 50,872 flat. Reading down the list the flat total falls and the percentage climbs, which is the whole story: the bigger your march, the more a percentage is worth and the less a flat bonus is.

Your march size

Optional. Fill it in and the range covering you is marked, and every range also shows the march you would end up with if you wore that gear.

It wants your base march size: before gear, and before the bonuses you ticked above. That is the number the ranges are measured in, so putting in your final march instead will point you at the wrong range.
